How It Works

From Low Level to Tank Full — Hands Off

Watch a full cycle: the tank drops, the pump kicks in, water rises, and the controller stops everything at the top mark.

Sensors Read the Level

Two probes sit in your tank — one at the low mark, one at the high mark. That's the entire sensing system.

Pump Starts on Low

When the level drops to the low probe, the controller closes the relay and your pump starts filling.

Stops at Tank Full

Water touches the high probe — pump off, Tank Full LED on. The tank can't overflow while you're asleep or out.

Protects & Recovers

Line Check stops a dry-running pump; after load shedding the box wakes up in AUTO and manages the next cycle itself.

FAQ

Smart Pump Questions

What is the Smart Pump controller?

Smart Pump is an automatic water pump controller. It reads your tank's water level and starts the pump when the tank is low, then stops it the moment the tank is full — so your pump runs only when it should, with no overflow and no burnt-out dry running.

Do I need to change my pump or wiring?

No. Smart Pump sits between your power supply and your existing pump. Your pump stays as it is, and the AUTO/MANUAL switch lets you take direct control whenever you want.

How does it know when the tank is full?

Level probes sit in your tank at the low and high marks. When water touches the high probe, the controller stops the pump and the Tank Full LED lights up. When the level drops to the low probe, it starts again — fully automatic.

What happens during load shedding?

The pump stops with the power, like any pump. When electricity returns, the controller wakes up in AUTO mode and manages the tank again — so an empty tank refills itself while you are out.
