FAQ

Router Power Bank Questions

What is a router power bank?

A compact 12V battery that plugs into your router's DC jack and keeps your Wi-Fi running 4–6 hours through load-shedding. It's smaller and completely silent — a practical UPS alternative when you only need your internet gear online.

Is a router power bank better than a UPS for Wi-Fi?

For just your router and ONT, usually yes. A router power bank is compact, silent and starts instantly, while a traditional UPS is bulkier, louder and built for whole-room loads like fans and monitors.

Which routers work with a 12V power bank?

Most PTCL, TP-Link, D-Link and fiber routers — plus Huawei and Netlink GPON ONTs — run on standard 12V DC. Send us your model on WhatsApp and we confirm compatibility in minutes.

Will my internet keep working during load-shedding?

Yes. As long as your router and ONT stay powered, your Wi-Fi stays up. The power bank carries the network gear while your phone and laptop run on their own batteries.
