FAQ

Solar Questions, Answered

How much does a solar system cost in Karachi?

System size depends on your electricity usage and roof space, so we don't publish one-size prices. Send us your recent K-Electric bill on WhatsApp and we'll recommend a size and send a quote the same day.

How much can solar reduce my electricity bill?

It depends on your daytime usage, system size and how much you export. With net metering, exported units are credited to your K-Electric account — our team estimates your savings from your actual bill before you commit.

What is net metering?

Net metering lets you export surplus solar units to the grid and receive credits on your electricity bill. We handle the system design and guide you through the K-Electric application process.

Do you install both residential and commercial systems?

Yes — homes, shops, offices and factories. Robotify has 30+ completed residential and commercial installations, from small home setups to larger commercial arrays.

Do you also supply equipment only?

Yes — we supply panels, inverters and balance-of-system components as well as turnkey installation. Message us on WhatsApp with your requirements.
