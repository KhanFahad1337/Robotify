FAQ

FYP Questions, Answered

What kinds of final year projects do you support?

Hardware and software. Arduino and ESP32 projects, IoT and automation, robotics, embedded systems, solar and power electronics, FPGA/VHDL digital-logic work — plus web and management systems, Android/iOS/Flutter apps, AI and machine learning, computer vision and data projects — with documentation.

Do you build software, mobile application and AI/ML projects too?

Yes — beyond hardware we build software and IT final year projects: Android, iOS and Flutter apps, web and management systems (PHP, Laravel, Python, MERN), AI and machine learning projects (TensorFlow/PyTorch, computer vision, NLP and chatbots), plus data and cybersecurity projects — with the same documentation and viva-prep support as our electronics builds.

Which languages and frameworks do you work in?

Common stacks we deliver in: Python, Java, C and C++, JavaScript with Node.js and React, PHP with Laravel, Flutter and React Native for mobile, plus TensorFlow and PyTorch for AI. Tell us your required stack in your first message and we'll confirm before quoting.

Do you help students outside Karachi?

Yes. We work with students across Pakistan over WhatsApp — we ship assembled hardware and keep you updated with photos and test videos through the build.

Will you teach me how my own project works?

Yes — every project includes a walkthrough so you can explain every block in your viva. We design the project to be understood by you, not just demonstrated by us.

Do you provide documentation and report help?

Yes — block diagrams, circuit schematics, component lists, bill of materials and report structure guidance are part of the package so your documentation matches your build.

How early should I start my FYP?

As early as possible — ideally 8 to 12 weeks before your deadline. Tight deadlines limit testing and documentation, so message us as soon as your topic is approved.
